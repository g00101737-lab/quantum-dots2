Sizes and colors follow the experimental CdSe sizing curve of Yu et al., Chem. Mater. 15, 2854 (2003), fitted for about 2 to 8 nm diameters. The curve is measured at the first absorption peak, and real emission sits a little redder. The energy levels are schematic: spacing is scaled to the band gap, and the wavefunctions are the lowest s-states of a spherical well, ψ ∝ sin(nπr/R)/r. Wavelength-to-color conversion approximates human vision.
